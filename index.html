A few things to know.

Do you translate or manage my account?

These packages use accurate Chinese source information supplied or approved by you. Foreign-language translation, account creation, posting, community management and advertising are not included.

Can you guarantee followers or sales?

No. The service delivers agreed content and operational materials. Reach, inquiries and sales depend on the offer, account, audience and distribution.

What should I prepare?

Your audience, product or service facts, authorized photos, style references and the deadline. AI visual reviews also need an approved character or style reference.

What if I need a different scope?

Send a brief. Extra posts, new themes or a change of direction receive a separate quote and timeline before work continues. Paid assets, subscriptions and third-party costs are agreed separately.
